Privacy Policy

RentMaster (xVantage Rental) · by Xvantage Infotech

Last updated: 10 July 2026

1. Introduction

Xvantage Infotech ("we", "us", "our") operates the RentMaster mobile application (also referred to as "xVantage Rental", the "App"), a property and tenant management platform for landlords and property owners ("App User", "you"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the App, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have.

This Privacy Policy is intended for and applies to the registered App User only — the landlord/property manager who creates an account and logs into the App. It is accessible within the App only after login, from the account menu.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Profile Information

The App uses phone number based login only (OTP verification). We collect:

  • Mobile phone number — used to send a one-time password (OTP) to verify and log you in; this is the only login method in the App
  • Name (first and last name)
  • Email address (optional, entered by you during profile creation, used for communication/invoices)
  • City, state, gender, and age (optional profile details)
  • Profile photo (optional)

We do not use Google or Facebook login in this App. No password is stored for your account — access is verified only via OTP sent to your registered phone number.

2.2 Property, Room & Tenant Data

As a landlord/property manager using the App, you may enter and store data about your properties and tenants, including:

  • Property details, addresses, room/unit types and photos
  • Tenant name, reference name, contact details, and room assignment
  • Rent amount, billing cycles, due dates, and payment/transaction history
  • Utility meter readings (electricity/water) and per-unit cost used for bill calculation
  • Tenant documents (e.g. ID proofs, agreements) uploaded as images/files
  • Invoices and statements generated within the App

Note: Tenant data is entered by the landlord/App user. The App user is responsible for having appropriate consent from their tenants to store this information.

2.4 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (device model, OS version, app version)
  • Push notification token (via Firebase) to send you reminders and alerts (e.g. rent due, payment received)
  • Basic usage/crash logs to help us fix bugs and improve the App

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you (OTP/social login)
  • To let you manage properties, rooms, tenants, rent, and payments
  • To generate invoices, statements, and payment receipts (PDF)
  • To send notifications such as due-payment reminders
  • To provide customer support and respond to feedback
  • To improve App performance, fix bugs, and develop new features
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the App and prevent misuse

We do not sell your personal information or your tenants' data to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on our own secured servers using industry-standard practices, including access-controlled databases and encrypted network transmission (HTTPS). Uploaded images/documents are stored on our server storage and linked to your account.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We may share limited data only in the following situations:

  • Service providers: Firebase (Google) for authentication, cloud messaging/push notifications, and app analytics/crash reporting.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or government request.
  • Business transfers: In case of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to users.

We do not share your data with advertisers, and the App does not display third-party ads.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account, property, and tenant data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 8).

7. Children's Privacy

RentMaster is intended for landlords and property managers who are 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & Correction: You can view and update most profile and property/tenant information directly within the App.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. We will process such requests within a reasonable time, subject to any legal record-keeping obligations.
  • Permissions: You can disable Camera, Contacts, Location, or Media permissions anytime from your device Settings. Some features may not work fully if permissions are denied.
  • Notifications: You can turn off push notifications from your device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
